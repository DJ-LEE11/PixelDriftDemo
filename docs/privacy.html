Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pixel Drift Demo (the “Game”), an independently developed mini game available through TikTok.

1. Current data practices

The current version of the Game does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Gameplay, score, and control input are processed locally for the current game session and are not sent to a server operated by the developer.

2. Information handled by TikTok

You access the Game through TikTok. TikTok may process account, device, diagnostic, usage, network, and other information under its own privacy policy and settings. That processing is controlled by TikTok, not by the developer of this Game. Please review TikTok’s privacy disclosures for details.

3. Hosting of these policy pages

These policy pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request logs as part of operating and securing its service. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement.

4. Cookies, analytics, advertising, and payments

The current version of the Game does not use developer-operated cookies, analytics, advertising, payment systems, or cross-service tracking. TikTok or GitHub may use their own technologies as described in their respective policies.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience arcade demonstration and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so the issue can be investigated and, where applicable, the information can be deleted.

6. Data retention and security

Because the current Game does not intentionally collect or store personal information on developer-operated servers, the developer has no such data to retain. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, and the security of TikTok and GitHub is governed by those providers.

7. International use

TikTok and GitHub may process information in countries other than your own. Please consult their policies for information about international transfers and applicable safeguards.

8. Future features

If a future version introduces login, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, payments, social features, or other collection of personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those practices begin, and any notices or consent required by applicable law will be provided.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights relating to personal information. The developer currently holds no intentionally collected personal information from the Game. Requests concerning information processed by TikTok or GitHub should be directed to the relevant provider.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Game or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
